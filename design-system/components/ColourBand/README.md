# ColourBand

A full-width section on `violet` (or `yellow`) that breaks the paper rhythm. Used for Radio Luft, Trackmate and the Retreats band.

- 2px `line` rules top and bottom; inner wrapper at `content-max`, padding `space-9`.
- Kicker in `on-violet`; display title in `white` with one word in `yellow`.
- Images get a 2px border and may tilt −2° and +2°. Three at most.
- Body text on violet is `on-violet`, never white.
- Never stack two colour bands back to back; put a paper section between them.
